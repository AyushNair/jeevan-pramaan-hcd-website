Experiment 3 · Ideation & Prototyping

Jeevan Pramaan: Redesigning Error Communication for Elderly Users

A Human-Centred Design Process project exploring how plain-language error messages can empower elderly pensioners to complete digital life certificate verification independently.

View Prototype Learn More
The Challenge

What is the problem?

India's Jeevan Pramaan system enables pensioners to digitally prove they are alive to keep receiving their pension. But when photo verification fails, the system shows only a cryptic "Verification Failed — Please try again" message. For elderly users like 82-year-old Viswan, who have limited digital literacy, this is deeply confusing and anxiety-inducing.

They don't know if the lighting was bad, their face wasn't positioned correctly, or something else went wrong. Without guidance, they retry the same mistake or give up entirely — forced to visit a pension office in person, which defeats the purpose of a digital system.

Our Approach

A human-centred solution

🔍

Understand the User

Through empathy mapping and persona building, we identified the core frustrations elderly pensioners face during digital verification — confusion, anxiety, and helplessness.

💡

Ideate Solutions

We brainstormed five potential approaches and evaluated them on feasibility, impact, user-friendliness, and implementation cost using a structured selection matrix.

✅

Prototype & Test

Our selected idea — plain-language rejection reasons with specific fix instructions — was prototyped as a working interactive demo with real camera integration.

Before & After

The difference clear error messages make

❌ Current System

Verification Failed

Please try again.

No explanation. No guidance. The user has no idea what went wrong or how to fix it.

✅ Our Redesign

Photo too dark

Move to a brighter spot, like near a window, and try again.

Specific reason + actionable instruction. The user knows exactly what to change.
