How Viswan (82) experiences this

💬 Says: "The photo looks fine to me — I don't know what's wrong with it."
🧠 Thinks: Believes the rejection is his own fault, since the system never explains the actual reason.
❤️ Feels: Anxiety about missing the deadline, and frustration after repeated failed attempts.
🖐️ Does: Retries the same photo several times, guessing at what to change, before asking a family member to take over.
With our redesign, Viswan sees "Photo too dark — move to a brighter spot" and knows exactly what to do. One clear instruction instead of confusion.

This prototype uses your real camera to measure ambient brightness. Results are based on actual lighting conditions — try it in different lighting to see how the error messages change.
